ABOUT US
Good Girls Bakery is a sourdough bread bakery offering artisan loaves, legendary English muffins, and decadent breakfast pastries located in Eagleview Town Center in Exton, PA.
Chef Sarah
Owner/Operator
Chef Sarah Holland is an experienced baker, chef and industry professional. Her love of baking prompted her to study and graduate from The Restaurant School of Walnut Hill College with a degree in the science of pastry arts (2010).

Her journey is driven by science and its relationship with food. Her ambition to succeed pushed her to evolve and learn different approaches to cuisine. Utilizing the scientific approach, Sarah’s dishes stand tall among the rest with bright flavors. She takes pride in using cooking and baking to create memories and experiences in her audiences.

Sarah’s career has informed her knowledge and experience stretching from food truck management, to French pastry, to cheffing at a Mexican restaurant and everything in between!

Her appetite for her craft is on the rise, just like her bread!
GRACE M.
Bakery Manager
Grace Matters is an experienced manager and licensed cosmetology professional with more than eight years of leadership experience in customer-focused businesses. She has a strong track record of leading teams, driving sales growth, developing employees, and overseeing daily operations in both the beauty and food service industries.

Currently serving as Bakery Manager at Good Girls Bakery, Grace combines operational expertise with a passion for customer service, staff development, and creating exceptional customer experiences. Her background includes managing high-performing teams, maintaining operational excellence, and fostering positive workplace cultures.

In addition to her penchant for supporting high-performing teams, Grace is an advocate for helping the unhoused community. She often partners with local shelters to provide cosmetology services and hand out free hygiene products to those experiencing homelessness.
